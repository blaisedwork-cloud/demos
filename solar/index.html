How we install

From roof check to switch-on.

Scroll and watch a system go together the way we install it: mounts, rails, panels row by row, then the inverter and battery, and the moment it switches on.

  1. Step 1 of 4Day 0

    Roof check

    Call or send the form and we'll look at your roof, your meter and your last power bill, then measure up. Free, and usually within the week.

  2. Step 2 of 4Install day, morning

    Mounts & rails

    Roof hooks fixed into the rafters, not the sheeting, then aluminium rails at engineered spans for Sunshine Coast wind ratings.

  3. Step 3 of 4Install day

    Panels on

    Panels clamped row by row, then the DC cabling runs down in conduit to the inverter. Most homes are done in one day.

  4. Step 4 of 4Same afternoon

    Switched on

    Inverter and battery on the wall, tested, and switched on with you there. We set up the app on your phone before we leave.
