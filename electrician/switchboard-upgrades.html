Signs your switchboard is due for an upgrade

  • It still has ceramic fuses with fuse wire.
  • There are no safety switches, or only one for the power points.
  • Fuses blow or breakers trip when a few appliances run at once.
  • You are renovating or adding something big, like air conditioning.

What we do

The owner comes out, looks at your switchboard and wiring, and explains what we found before we fix anything. You get the price before we start. The new board goes in with circuit breakers and safety switches, and we clean up when we finish.

Do I need a new switchboard to add safety switches?

Not always. Some boards have room for safety switches and some need replacing. We will tell you which after a look.
