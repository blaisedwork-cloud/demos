How it works

From first call to final invoice

No surprises. Here’s exactly what happens.

  1. 1

    Call or send a request

    Ring 0491 570 157 or fill in the quote form. Tell us what’s happening and where you are.

  2. 2

    The owner comes out

    Our call-out fee is $110 including GST, which covers travel and the first 20 minutes.

  3. 3

    We explain what we found

    Before we fix anything, you hear what the problem is and what it’ll take. Switchboard upgrades are quoted on inspection.

  4. 4

    We fix it and clean up

    The work gets done, and we always clean up after ourselves.

  5. 5

    Pay on completion

    Card or bank transfer once the job is done. We send the invoice by email.
