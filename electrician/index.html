Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Yes, our call-out fee is $110 including GST, which covers travel and the first 20 minutes.

My safety switch keeps tripping. What should I do?

Unplug appliances on that circuit and try resetting it. If it still trips, call us. Don't keep resetting it or open the switchboard yourself.

Do you do commercial work?

No, we only do residential work.

How do I pay?

Card or bank transfer on completion. We send the invoice by email.

Do you install EV chargers?

Not at the moment. We can recommend someone who does.

Which areas do you cover?

Do you do emergency call-outs?

Are your quotes free?
