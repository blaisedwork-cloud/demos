Switchboard upgrade · start to finish

Old board out. Lights back on.

Keep scrolling and the roof comes off. Watch a typical switchboard upgrade, from finding the fault to every room switched back on.

  1. Step 1 of 4Same week

    Find the fault

    Lights flickering, a safety switch that keeps tripping, or an old ceramic fuse board? Sam comes out, tests every circuit, shows you what's failing and gives you a fixed price on the spot.

  2. Step 2 of 4Install day · 4 to 6 hours

    New switchboard

    Power off, old board out, new board in: a main switch, surge protection and a safety switch (RCBO) on every circuit.

  3. Step 3 of 4Same day

    New circuits

    New cable run through the roof to the lights, power points and the EV charger in the garage, each on its own protected circuit.

  4. Step 4 of 4Before we leave

    Lights back on

    Every circuit and safety switch tested and recorded, then the power goes back on room by room. You get the compliance certificate and a board labelled so you can actually read it.
