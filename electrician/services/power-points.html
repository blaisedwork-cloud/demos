Power points in Penrith

Where people usually want more power

  • Kitchen benches, for the appliances that now live there.
  • Bedsides and desks, with USB outlets for phones and laptops.
  • Behind the TV, where the power board always ends up.
  • Outside: the patio, the shed or the barbecue area.

Power boards and double adaptors are handy, but they aren’t meant to be permanent. A proper power point where you need it is tidier and safer.

Inside or outside

Outdoor power points need to be weatherproof and on a circuit protected by a safety switch. If your board doesn’t have one, we’ll explain your options, including safety switch installation.
