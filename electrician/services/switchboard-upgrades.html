Switchboard upgrades in Penrith

Plenty of older Penrith homes still run on ceramic fuses. We replace them with a modern switchboard and safety switches, and walk you through it first.

Get a Free Quote Call 0491 570 157

Signs your switchboard is due

  • It still has ceramic fuses (the old pull-out kind with fuse wire).
  • There are no safety switches, or not enough to cover your circuits.
  • Fuses blow or power drops out more often than it should.
  • The meter box is rusty, cracked or crowded with old gear.
  • You’re planning a renovation or adding new circuits.

Scorch marks, a burning smell or a buzzing board? Don’t wait for a quote. Call 0491 570 157.

How a switchboard upgrade works

  1. We look first. The owner comes out and checks the board, the meter box and the wiring behind it.
  2. We explain what we found. You get a plain-English rundown and a price before any work starts.
  3. We do the work. Old fuses out, new board and safety switches in.
  4. We clean up. We leave the place the way we found it, minus the old board.

Why it’s quote on inspection

Every old board is different. The size of the board, the state of the wiring and the meter box all change the job, so we price it once we’ve seen it rather than guess over the phone.
