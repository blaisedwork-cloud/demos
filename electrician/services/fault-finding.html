Problems we track down

  • A safety switch that trips again and again.
  • Power points or lights that have stopped working.
  • A whole circuit that has gone dead.
  • Power dropping out when a certain appliance runs.

Before you call: safety switch keeps tripping?

Unplug appliances on that circuit and try resetting it. If it still trips, call us. Don’t keep resetting it or open the switchboard yourself.

Lost power completely, or something is sparking, smoking or hot? That’s an emergency call-out. Call 0491 570 157. If anyone is hurt or there’s a fire, call 000 first.

What it costs

Our call-out fee is $110 including GST, which covers travel and the first 20 minutes. Plenty of faults are found in that time. If it’s a bigger job, we explain what we found and what the fix will cost before we go further.
