This policy explains how Tallowwood Electrical ("we") handles personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

This website does not use contact forms, accounts or advertising trackers. If you phone or email us, we receive the details you choose to give us (such as your name, phone number, email, address and job details).

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, quote, carry out and invoice work, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Website hosting

Our website host may automatically record technical information such as IP addresses and browser type for security and performance. We do not use this to identify you.

Website assistant

The assistant on this website answers questions using only information published on this site. It runs in your browser and does not send or store the questions you type. Please do not enter personal, health or payment details into it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing jobs@example.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
