Signs your hot water system needs a look

  • No hot water, or it runs out much faster than it used to.
  • Water leaking from the tank, the pipes or the relief valve.
  • Rusty or discoloured hot water.
  • On a gas system, the pilot light keeps going out.

Repair or replace

We inspect your system and quote before any work starts. Sometimes a repair is all it needs, sometimes a new system makes more sense. We work on electric, gas and heat-pump systems.
