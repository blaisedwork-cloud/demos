How we handle the details you send through our quote form.

What we collect

When you use our quote form we collect your name, phone number, email address, suburb, the service you need, your preferred contact time and the message you write.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry, give you a price and book in the work. We do not sell your details or add you to a mailing list.

How it is sent and stored

The form is delivered to our email by Web3Forms, a form delivery service. Your enquiry is kept in our email for as long as we need it for the job and our records.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email hello@example.com or call 0491 570 156.
