Fair questions

Straight answers.

I get enough work from word of mouth.

Good on you. Word of mouth is the best work there is. But when someone gets your name from a mate, most will look you up before they ring. A proper site shows them you're real, licensed and local, and puts your number one tap away.

I've got a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?

Keep it. A website sits alongside it. It's the place that's yours: your own web address, your licence and ABN, a page for each job you do and a quote form that lands in your email. Not everyone's on Facebook, and you don't control how it shows your business.

What do I have to do?

Not much. A 15-minute video call, then a 5-minute form on your phone (mostly tapping): your jobs, suburbs, hours, licence and photos. Then you check the preview and tell me what to change. I do the rest.

How long does it take?

You'll see a preview in about a week after you send the form. Two rounds of changes are included. Once you say "approved", it goes live.

Who owns it?

You do. It goes live on your own web address, registered in your name and paid by you straight to the registrar (a .com.au is about $10 for the first year).

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. There's no deposit. You see the finished site, you get two rounds of changes, and you only pay the $990 once you're happy and say "approved".

Are there ongoing costs?

Only your web address (about $10 for the first year for a .com.au). The monthly plans are optional: Essentials $39 a month or Standard $69 a month, month to month, cancel any time by email. Or no plan at all: I set it up on your own free hosting so it runs without me.

Will it get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise that, and I won't. What you get is a proper, fast site with the basics Google looks for: clear pages for each job, the areas you cover, and your business details. SEO and ads aren't part of the $990.

Why so cheap compared with agencies?

I'm small and local, I only build for tradies, and there are no account managers or big office to pay for. You deal with me, start to finish.
