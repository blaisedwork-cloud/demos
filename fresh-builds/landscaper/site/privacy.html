Privacy

The short version: we only collect what we need to reply to your quote request, and we don't sell or share it.

What the quote form collects

Your name, phone number, email address, suburb, the service you're after, your preferred contact time and anything you write in the message box.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your quote, give you a price and organise the work. We ask for your suburb so we know you're in our area.

How it's sent and stored

The form is delivered to our email through Web3Forms, a form service. We keep your details in our email and job records only as long as we need them for your work and our tax records.

Who sees it

Just the two of us. We don't sell, rent or share your details with marketers.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email hello@example.com or call 0491 570 158.
