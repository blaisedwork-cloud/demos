Our story

Small team, on purpose

Greenline Garden Care is a landscaper team based in Castle Hill. We keep it to two people so the same faces turn up at your place every time, and so we know how you like your lawn and where the tricky edges are.

Most of our week is regular maintenance: mowing, edging, hedging and clean-ups. In between we take on small landscaping jobs like laying new turf.
