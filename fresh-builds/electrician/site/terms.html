Information on this site

We try to keep everything here accurate and up to date, but it’s general information only. Prices shown are starting prices and include GST. Your actual price is confirmed in your quote.

Electrical safety

Nothing on this site is a guide to doing electrical work yourself. Electrical work must be done by a licensed electrician. If you think something is unsafe, turn off the power if it’s safe to do so, and call us. If anyone is hurt or there’s a fire, call 000.

Quotes and bookings

Sending the quote form doesn’t book a job or create a contract. A job is booked once we’ve agreed on it with you.
