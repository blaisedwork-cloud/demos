Safety switch installation in Penrith

A safety switch watches the power on a circuit and cuts it in a split second if something goes wrong. Older homes often have none, or too few.

Get a Free Quote Call 0491 570 157

What a safety switch does

Circuit breakers protect the wiring. Safety switches (also called RCDs) protect people. If power starts leaking somewhere it shouldn’t, like through a faulty appliance or a person, the safety switch trips and the power goes off.

When to add safety switches

  • Your switchboard has no safety switches, or only one for the whole house.
  • You’ve got kids, or you use power tools and appliances outside.
  • You’re buying, renting out or renovating an older home.

If your board still has ceramic fuses, a switchboard upgrade may be the better fix. We’ll tell you which makes sense.

My safety switch keeps tripping. What should I do?

Unplug appliances on that circuit and try resetting it. If it still trips, call us. Don’t keep resetting it or open the switchboard yourself. Tracking that down is our fault finding service.
