Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

This policy explains how Greenline Garden Care ("we") handles personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

This website does not use accounts or advertising trackers. If you phone, text or email us, or use the enquiry form, we receive the details you choose to give us (such as your name, phone number, suburb, address and job details).

The enquiry form

If you use the form on our contact page, we receive what you type in it: your name, phone number, suburb, the job, how soon you need it and any details you add. The form is sent by Web3Forms, a form service, which emails it to us. Web3Forms keeps a copy on its servers, which may be outside Australia, as set out in its own privacy policy: web3forms.com/privacy.

Please don't put sensitive information, such as health or payment details, in the form.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, quote, carry out and invoice work, and meet our legal obligations. We keep them only as long as we need them for the job and our business records. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Website hosting

Our website host may automatically record technical information such as IP addresses and browser type for security and performance. We do not use this to identify you.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing hello@example.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
