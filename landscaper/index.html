Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need an engineer for my wall?

Walls over 1 m, or near a boundary or building, usually do. We organise it and include it in the quote.

How long until I can walk on the turf?

Light use after about two weeks, once it's rooted. We'll tell you exactly how to water it.

Do you take the old stuff away?

Yes. Spoil, old turf and rubbish are removed and priced in the quote.
